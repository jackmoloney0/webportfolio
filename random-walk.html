Random Walk Simulator

Stochastic Price Dynamics & Monte Carlo Uncertainty

Overview

This simulator models a discrete-time random walk for a synthetic asset price. Each step applies a stochastic shock drawn from a Gaussian distribution, allowing prices to evolve along many possible paths. By generating hundreds of trajectories simultaneously, the system visually demonstrates how randomness compounds into uncertainty.

How It Works

1. Initialise

Define step count, number of paths, starting value S₀, and volatility level.

2. Generate Random Shocks

Each step adds Gaussian noise: ε ~ N(0, σ). Paths evolve independently using additive updates.

3. Analyse Spread

Compare trajectories and examine the distribution of final prices to understand how uncertainty compounds over time.

Built with Python, NumPy, and Matplotlib, designed for quantitative experimentation and stochastic intuition.
