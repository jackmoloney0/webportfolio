Random Walk Simulator

Stochastic Price Dynamics & Monte Carlo Uncertainty

Overview

This simulator models a discrete-time random walk for an asset price. Each step applies Gaussian noise, producing many possible price paths that illustrate how uncertainty compounds over time. By simulating hundreds of trajectories, the model visualises the probabilistic spread inherent in stochastic systems.
