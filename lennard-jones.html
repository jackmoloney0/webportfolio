Lennard-Jones Simulation

Molecular Dynamics • Inter-Particle Forces • Potential Energy

Overview

This simulation models interacting particles governed by the classical Lennard-Jones potential. The LJ force captures both the short-range repulsion (when particles get too close) and longer-range attraction, producing realistic molecular clustering and stability.

By integrating particle motion over time, the system reveals how energy minimisation naturally leads to equilibrium spacing and emergent structure.

How It Works

1. Compute Forces

For every particle pair, the Lennard-Jones formula determines repulsion or attraction based on separation distance.

2. Integrate Motion

A numerical scheme (Euler / Verlet) updates particle velocities and positions at each timestep.

3. Observe Equilibrium

The system evolves toward lower-energy states, producing stable particle spacing and emergent structures.

Developed with Python, NumPy and Matplotlib, applying classical molecular-dynamics techniques.
