Lennard-Jones Particle Simulation

A molecular dynamics model of interacting particles governed by the Lennard-Jones potential.

Overview

This project simulates particles moving under the Lennard-Jones potential — a classical model combining short-range repulsion and longer-range attraction. The system showcases realistic molecular behaviour such as clustering, equilibrium spacing, and energy minimisation.

Dynamic Trajectories

How the Simulation Works

1. Force Model

The Lennard-Jones potential is defined and used to compute pairwise forces between particles based on their separation distances.

2. Time Integration

Using a numerical integration scheme (e.g. Verlet or Euler), positions and velocities are updated step-by-step to evolve the system.

3. Energy & Stability

Over time, the system tends towards lower energy configurations, demonstrating clustering and stable structures around the potential minimum.
